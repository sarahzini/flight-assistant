Baggage allowance depends on the airline and fare class. Economy fares typically
include one carry-on bag (max 8kg) and one checked bag (max 23kg). Excess baggage
fees apply per extra kilogram or per extra bag, and vary by airline. Liquids in
carry-on luggage must be in containers of 100ml or less, placed in a single
transparent bag. Sporting equipment and musical instruments usually require
special handling and advance notice to the airline.
