A layover (or connection) is a stop between two flights on the same journey,
where passengers change planes without leaving the airport system entirely.
A short layover is typically under 90 minutes and carries higher risk of
missing the next flight if the first one is delayed. If a passenger misses
their connecting flight because the airline's own earlier flight was delayed,
the airline is usually responsible for rebooking them onto the next available
flight at no extra cost. International connections often require passing
through security or immigration again, depending on the airports involved.
