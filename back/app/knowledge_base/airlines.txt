Airlines are identified by a two-letter IATA code and typically operate
under either a full-service model (checked baggage, meals, and seat
selection included) or a low-cost model (fees charged separately for most
extras). Full-service and low-cost carriers alike publish a "conditions of
carriage" document that governs refunds, changes, and liability, which
passengers implicitly accept when purchasing a ticket. Alliances (such as
Star Alliance, SkyTeam, or Oneworld) let passengers earn and use frequent
flyer benefits across partner airlines, and codeshare agreements mean a
single itinerary can involve flights physically operated by a different
airline than the one it was booked with.
